# Nexum

But seriously though: “What you say to yourself becomes what you live. So… _**What story are you telling?**_”

<details>

<summary><strong>🫥What is CommIT?</strong></summary>

CommIT is a living, evolving system designed to refine thought, behavior, and action through storytelling. It's not a productivity method. It's not a philosophy. It's not a religion. Hell it could be a cult but it doesn't like it. It's a Cycle—a structured way of authorship to existing that constantly improves itself through alignment, iteration, and context-responsiveness and narration. Basically, it's a can of worms but you can actually eat it. A damn feedback loop that is self aware and is allergic to bullsh\*t.

You don’t "use" CommIT. You enter into a relationship with it. You grow alongside it. You don’t read CommIT to understand it—you practice it, and it reveals itself. So however you live with it is different, so- it literally changes meaning for everyone individually. There is no one right meaning because it's meaning changes over time.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>🎭What is Nexum?</strong></summary>

Nexum is the personal implementation of CommIT. It’s not a simplified version of the system—it’s the bridge between who you are now and the complexity you are capable of growing into.

Understanding the story:

* To yourself (who you think you are)
* To others (how you explain yourself, your past, your pain, your plans)
* And how those stories affect your choices, your emotions, and your future

We all live in stories.\
Nexum teaches you how to notice them, question them, and rewrite them—again and again, to anchor you just enough to begin. You’ll learn to think in Cycles/Loops. To iterate, not stagnate. To align your behavior with context, rather than force context to conform to your assumptions.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>🪢Why CommIT Exists</strong></summary>

Because systems fail when humans refuse to refine. Because brilliance without iteration decays. Because intelligence isn't enough—we need structural integrity in how we live, think, and act. Instead of complaining, CommIT actually gives people the tools to do something about something. We are f\*cked. But we can do something about it. Here are the tools, and how it can be used. I won’t tell you what to do with your life, use these tools to do something yourself.

CommIT is not here to help you feel good. It’s here to help you become accurate. And aligned. And functional across shifting environments. It was never about “here’s how you should do it”, rather, it's about “you know a lot of shit. So- what now? What are you gonna do about it?”

It was built from lived experience, intellectual rigor, emotional collapse, existential rebellion, and above all—the refusal to let chaos win without meaning.

</details>

<summary><strong>🧭 The Three Layers of Nexum</strong></summary>

**1. Your Inner Story**

What do you believe about yourself?\
Are you a failure? A fighter? A burden? A genius?\
Where did that idea come from—and is it still true?

Loop to see the following:

* Spot old stories that aren’t helping anymore
* Figure out where they came from
* Practice telling them in a better way—one that helps you move, not stay stuck

***

**2. The Story You Share**

What do you tell other people?\
Are you honest? Do you perform? Do you hide parts of the truth?

See the following:

* Are you telling stories just to be liked, safe, or impressive?
* Speak what's real—not exaggerated, not minimized
* Handle how people react to your story, without changing yourself just to please them

Because no one can tell a better story about you than yourself. You are the main character of your story. Own that shit.

***

**3. The Loop**

Every story you tell feeds back into your life.

Example:

_<mark style="background-color:red;">**You tell yourself you’re bad at relationships → You avoid intimacy → You feel lonely → You believe your story even more→You continue wanting to be alone**</mark>_

This is a Loop. And this is a self-destructive one. Not only are you making limits for yourself, you are also limiting other people to you. It’s not about removing boundaries, it's about putting them consciously.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>🧠 A Tool You Can Use — The “Story Check”</strong></summary>

Whenever something feels off, ask:

1. What’s the story I’m telling myself right now?
2. Where did I learn it?
3. Is it true right now?
4. What is this story making me do or avoid?
5. What might a clearer story sound like?

***

Examples:

“No one cares what I have to say.”\
Story Check\
→ Origin: Realize it came from high school bullying\
→ Reality: Not actually true anymore\
→ Clearer story: “I still fear being ignored, but some people do listen now. I can speak anyway.”

“I always mess things up.”

Story Check\
→ Origin: Childhood mistakes that got harsh reactions from adults\
→ Reality: You’ve succeeded many times, but you ignore those moments\
→ Clearer story: “I’m afraid of failing because of how it used to feel—but now I have more tools and support. One mistake doesn’t define me.”

“If I ask for help, I’m weak.”

Story Check\
→ Origin: Grew up being praised for being “independent” or punished for needing things\
→ Reality: Struggling alone often makes things worse\
→ Clearer story: “Asking for help is something strong people do when they want to grow. It’s a skill, not a flaw.”

“I have to be useful or people won’t keep me around.”

Story Check\
→ Origin: Felt disposable or ignored unless performing or producing value\
→ Reality: People have stayed for who you are, not just what you do\
→ Clearer story: “Being useful can feel good, but I don’t have to earn my right to exist. I matter even when I rest.”

“I’m too much for people.”\
Story Check\
→ Origin: Past rejection for being intense, emotional, smart, or different\
→ Reality: Some people have made you feel like that—but others appreciate your depth\
→ Clearer story: “I’m not ‘too much.’ I just haven’t always been in the right rooms. The right people can handle me—and some even need my intensity.”

“I should be further ahead by now.”

Story Check\
→ Origin: Comparison to others, pressure to perform, internalized timelines\
→ Reality: Your path is non-linear, and you’ve had real reasons for detours\
→ Clearer story: “I’ve grown in ways others haven’t seen. I’m not behind—I’m building something that takes time.”

<summary><strong>🧭 Shared Reality Check – Responsibility Beyond the Self</strong></summary>

“Your story is yours to rewrite—but **you are not the only one living in it.**”

Owning your narrative is powerful, but it doesn’t make you exempt from impact.\
Clarity is not a free pass for harm.

* You can choose to tell yourself, “I’m finally being authentic.”
* But if your version of “authenticity” involves blaming, manipulating, or harming others to protect your own narrative, it’s no longer growth—it’s theater.

**Every rewritten story has a ripple.**\
&#xNAN;_**Healthy stories don’t demand that others become villains just so you can feel like the hero.**_
